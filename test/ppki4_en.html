Guidelines for Writing Scientific Paper

IPB University

2020

Scientific Journals

  • One author: (Syuaib 2015; Kolopaking 2016)
  • Two authors: (Sutiawan and Hernowo 2017)
  • Three to ten authors: (Darusman et al. 2014)
  • More than ten authors: (Kusrini et al. 2017)
  • Author is an organization: (SSCCCP 1976)
  • Article without author’s name: (Tren… 2006)
  • Editorial: (Hunter and Duff 2016)
  • Short communication: (Wibowo et al. 2017)
  • Research note: (Darmadi et al. 2002)
  • Feedback article: (Ilyas 2006)
  • Review article: (Gunawan 1997)
  • Article with broken pages: (Crews and Garstka 1981)
  • Article in an insert, supplement, or special edition: (Rifai 1992)
  • Article title translated into English: (Ismayana et al. 2017)
  • Reprinted article: (Young 1987)
  • Article accepted for publication, but not yet published: (Satria et al. 2017)
  • Article from the internet: (Kolopaking 2016; Baez et al. 2017)

Books

  • Book: (Dyball and Newell 2015; Satria 2015)
  • Book with editors: (Manuwoto et al. 2015)
  • Book with an institution or organization as author: (IPB 2017; IPB 2019)
  • Translated book without editors: (Pelczar and Chan 1986)
  • Translated book with editors: (Hart et al. 2003)
  • Book series with the same volume title: (Wijayakusuma et al. 1998)
  • Book series with different volume titles: (Phillips and Hardy 2002)
  • Chapter in a book: (Buchori et al. 2017)
  • Chapter in a book series with different volume titles: (Wahyuni 2007)

Conference Proceedings

  • Article in proceedings: (Nurtjahya et al. 2011)
  • Article in proceedings downloaded from the internet: (Leiwakabessy et al. 2017)
  • Abstract in proceedings: (Rahayu 2010)

Theses

  • Undergraduate thesis (skripsi): (Febrizal 2017)
  • Master’s thesis (tesis): (Palupi 2016)
  • Doctoral dissertation (disertasi): (Amelia 2017)
