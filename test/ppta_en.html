Guidelines for Writing Final Assignment

IPB University

2026

Scientific Journals

  • One author, issue numbered: (Syed 2024)
  • One author, issue not numbered: (Brett 2025)
  • Two authors: (Rusmana and Nedwell 2024)
  • Three to five authors: (Aulia et al. 2024)
  • More than five authors: (Riyanto et al. 2025)
  • Author is an organization: (CPPS 2019)
  • Organization with full name (Extra: Authority): (SSCCCP 1976)
  • Article without author’s name: (Tren… 2026)
  • Editorial: (Hunter and Duff 2016)
  • Short communication: (Wibowo et al. 2017)
  • Review article: (Lee et al. 2026)
  • Article title translated into English: (Ismayana et al. 2017)
  • Insert or supplement issue: (Rifai 1992)
  • Reprinted article (Extra: References): (Young 1987)
  • Accepted for publication, not yet published: (Resmeiliana et al. 2025)

Books

  • Book: (Arifin and Kaswanto 2023; Budde and McCluskey 2023)
  • Book with edition: (Rusli 2012)
  • Book with editor: (Wahyudi et al. 2022)
  • Book with an institution as author: (IPB 2026)
  • Translated book: (Higuchi 2016)
  • Book series with different volume titles: (Kingdon et al. 2013)
  • Chapter in a book: (Buchori et al. 2017)
  • Chapter in a book series: (Bellefontaine and Johansson 2016)

Conference Proceedings

  • Article in proceedings: (Yunindanova et al. 2015)
  • Article in proceedings from the internet: (Mirsam et al. 2015)
  • Abstract in proceedings: (Rahayu 2010)

Theses

  • Undergraduate thesis: (Fadillah 2024)
  • Master’s thesis: (Efendi 2024)
  • Dissertation: (Dewi 2025)
